Your account

Your Riot ID is the name and tag shown in the Valorant client, like TenZ#0505. LATAM and BR players can also pick NA.

Add it to OBS

  1. Copy your link.
  2. In OBS, add a Browser source.
  3. Paste it into URL, set Width to 640 and Height to 200, and click OK.

Today counts the games since you started playing, and starts over by itself after 3 hours with no ranked games. To start it over yourself, right-click the source, pick Interact and click Reset in the corner of the card.

RR updates a minute or two after each game ends.

Changed something? Copy the link again and paste it over the old one.
